# Demo Script (3 Minutes)

**[0:00 - 0:30] Introduction & Dashboard**
"Hello, we are team IntoTheSpace. For the 'Flame in Freefall' challenge, we tackled a critical problem: How do we turn decades of complex NASA combustion research into actionable fire-safety insights for spacecraft engineers? Welcome to our platform."
*(Show the dashboard. Point out the dark, modern UI, the metrics, and the charts.)*
"Here on the dashboard, engineers get an immediate overview of available research, from methane experiments to hydrogen burn data."

**[0:30 - 1:00] Explorer & Details**
"Let's say an engineer is designing a habitat and needs to know about methane combustion."
*(Click 'Explore Research', type 'methane' in the search bar, click on the FLEX-1 experiment.)*
"They can instantly pull up the experimental conditions—fuel, gravity, oxygen concentration—and read the exact observed behavior, all sourced directly from the data."

**[1:00 - 1:45] AI Research (RAG)**
"But reading raw reports is slow. We built an AI Research Assistant using Retrieval-Augmented Generation."
*(Navigate to AI Research. Type: "How does microgravity affect flame stability?")*
"Notice how the AI doesn't just guess. It retrieves the relevant documents from our database, synthesizes an answer, and—crucially—cites its sources right below. This ensures scientific integrity."

**[1:45 - 2:30] Compare & Insights**
"What if we want to compare two environments?"
*(Navigate to Compare, select 'FLEX-1 Methane' and 'Normal Gravity Hydrogen', click Compare.)*
"Our engine instantly builds a side-by-side comparison and generates an AI summary of the differences."
*(Navigate to Insights)*
"Finally, the platform automatically extracts high-level 'Fire Safety Insights'—like the fact that spherical flames are more stable in microgravity—giving engineers immediate, actionable intelligence."

**[2:30 - 3:00] Conclusion**
"IntoTheSpace bridges the gap between raw NASA science and applied engineering. It runs locally, uses modular AI, and is ready to help design the next generation of safe spacecraft. Thank you."
